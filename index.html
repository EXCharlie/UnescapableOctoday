Dates: March 2 – March 9, 2025
Location: Main Teaching Building, Class 8, Grade 10 (Class of 2026)

A small spark of inspiration emerged during class — a time loop trap. The concept fascinated me immediately. I had long wanted to gather classmates to film a short film, and video production was an entirely new field for me. After discussing the idea with several peers, everyone agreed the story was worth making. The team was deeply united: creative members shared ideas, willing actors sacrificed their weekends for shooting, and a physics teacher also made a guest appearance. Together, we completed the final film.

The Unescapable Eighth Day of the Week was originally submitted for the school’s Science and Culture Art Festival. Few expected Class 8, Grade 10 to produce a work of this scale. After completion, the short film was temporarily taken offline by the school administration over concerns of potential adverse impacts. No awards were given out for the festival that year.

Story

When the Saturday school dismissal bell rings, mobile-game-addicted teen Gu Xingwei hurries home with his classmate for a duo ranked game, only to be trapped in an endlessly looping teaching building.

He suffers destroyed homework, fruitless attempts to seek help, and witnesses bullying. As he empathizes with victim Z, memory flashbacks expose that he was once the bully. This temporal cage gives him an opportunity to confront himself and achieve redemption.
